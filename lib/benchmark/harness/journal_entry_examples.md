# Journal Entry Examples

## 1. Cash sale

Sold a shirt for ₦2,000, paid immediately.

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Cash | 2,000 | | Asset increased (cash received) |
| Income | Sales | | 2,000 | Income increased (earned by the sale) |

## 2. Credit sale

Sold a shirt for ₦2,000, customer pays later.

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Accounts Receivable | 2,000 | | Asset increased (business is now owed money) |
| Income | Sales | | 2,000 | Income increased (sale already earned, regardless of when paid) |

## 3. Customer pays a prior credit sale

Customer pays the ₦2,000 owed from Example 2.

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Cash | 2,000 | | Asset increased (cash received) |
| Asset | Accounts Receivable | | 2,000 | Asset decreased (the amount owed is now settled) |

## 4. Purchase on credit

## 17. Importing goods in USD with an exchange rate move

Ordered $1,000 of goods from China on credit (rate ₦1,500/$), paid ₦80,000 freight/customs in cash, then paid the supplier later when the rate moved to ₦1,550/$.

**Goods received:**

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Inventory | 1,500,000 | | Asset increased (goods now held, valued at rate on order date) |
| Liability | Accounts Payable (USD) | | 1,500,000 | Liability increased (owed to supplier, recorded in Naira at order-date rate) |

**Freight and customs paid:**

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Inventory | 80,000 | | Asset increased (cost added to bring goods to usable condition) |
| Asset | Cash | | 80,000 | Asset decreased (cash paid out) |

**Supplier paid later, rate now ₦1,550/$:**

| Base Type | Account | Debit | Credit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liability | Accounts Payable (USD) | 1,500,000 | | Liability decreased (original recorded debt cleared) |
| Expense | FX Loss | 50,000 | | Expense increased (extra Naira needed purely from the rate moving against you) |
| Asset | Cash | | 1,550,000 | Asset decreased (actual Naira paid out, at the new rate) |
